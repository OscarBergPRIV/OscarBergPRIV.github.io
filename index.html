About Me

I'm Oscar Berg, a double degree PhD student at TU Wien (Austria) and Mid Sweden University (Sweden), specializing in efficient AI implementation. My research focuses on developing innovative strategies for AI model partitioning to optimize performance and resource utilization.

My passion spans from Large Language Models and agentic AI systems to efficient inference and intelligent automation. I'm particularly fascinated by how LLMs can be leveraged to build autonomous agents that reason, plan, and execute complex tasks — and how these models can be deployed efficiently at scale.

My background is rooted in embedded AI. During my Master's thesis at Fraunhofer IMS (Duisburg), I designed and built tinyHLS — a template-based compiler enabling TensorFlow-to-Verilog translation for embedded AI deployment. The thesis was graded 1.0, and tinyHLS is now under active development and maintenance by Fraunhofer IMS.
